A fully sampled spiral image needs 35 arms. We collect only 3 per frame, which is fast enough to follow the airway as it collapses but leaves the raw image unreadable. Our self-supervised model recovers a clean image from those 3 arms.

Research

I develop self-supervised deep learning methods for accelerated dynamic MRI. My PhD work uses spiral variational manifold learning to image upper-airway collapse in patients with obstructive sleep apnea, including during natural sleep.

This research is supported by NIH NHLBI grant R01 HL173483 and NIH-S10 instrumentation grant 1S10OD025025-01.

Accelerated dynamic upper-airway MRI

QDMRI Lab, The University of Iowa · 2024 to present

Self-supervised spiral variational manifold learning for highly undersampled dynamic MRI, used to image where and how the pharyngeal airway collapses in sleep apnea. The work spans method development, sensitivity analysis, prospective validation, and multi-slice imaging.

Deep learning for medical image classification

Bio-Imaging Research Lab, Islamic University, Bangladesh · 2020 to 2024

Explainable deep learning for disease detection from images and signals, including skin cancer, brain tumors, Alzheimer's disease, lung abnormalities, COVID-19, monkeypox, colon cancer, eye disease, and atrial fibrillation.
