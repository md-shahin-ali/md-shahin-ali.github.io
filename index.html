Research

In obstructive sleep apnea (OSA), the upper airway closes during sleep as the tongue and the soft palate (velum) lose muscle tone and collapse into the airway.

A newer treatment, hypoglossal nerve stimulation (HNS), implants a device that senses breathing and stimulates the nerve controlling the base of the tongue during each inhalation, pushing the tongue forward to keep the airway open. It frees patients from wearing a CPAP mask all night, but it does not work for everyone. Patients whose airway collapses front to back respond well, while patients with circular (concentric) collapse have high failure rates. Choosing the right patients depends on seeing exactly how each airway collapses.

Today: drug-induced sleep endoscopy

  • Invasive
  • Performed under anesthesia
  • Collapse depends on the drug dose
  • High variability between readers
  • Cannot view all collapse sites at once

Our approach: dynamic MRI

  • Non-invasive
  • Works during natural sleep
  • Clear soft-tissue contrast
  • Covers the whole airway, velopharynx to hypopharynx, at once
  • Lets us measure the collapse quantitatively

Our lab develops MRI methods to measure these airway dynamics. I develop self-supervised deep learning methods for accelerated dynamic MRI: my PhD work uses spiral variational manifold learning to image upper-airway collapse in patients with OSA, including during natural sleep.

Accelerated dynamic upper-airway MRI

QDMRI Lab, Roy J. Carver Department of Biomedical Engineering, The University of Iowa · 2024 to present

Self-supervised spiral variational manifold learning for highly undersampled dynamic MRI, used to image where and how the pharyngeal airway collapses in sleep apnea. The work spans method development, sensitivity analysis, prospective validation, and multi-slice imaging.

Bio-Imaging Research Lab, Islamic University, Bangladesh · 2020 to 2024

Explainable deep learning for disease detection from images and signals, including skin cancer, brain tumors, Alzheimer's disease, lung abnormalities, COVID-19, monkeypox, colon cancer, eye disease, and atrial fibrillation.
